> This section compiles technical knowledge-sharing posts and study logs focusing on **AWS Cloud Security** that I published in the [AWS Study Group VN](https://www.facebook.com/groups/660548818043427/user/100016726392723) community during the **First Cloud AI Journey** internship.

### [Blog 1 - Exploring the Shared Responsibility Model and Amazon S3 Data Security](3.1-Blog1/)
A structured study log examining the **AWS Shared Responsibility Model** across Amazon EC2 and Amazon S3, focusing on foundational S3 data protection mechanisms including **S3 Block Public Access**, default **SSE-S3** encryption at rest, and **S3 Versioning** with storage cost trade-offs.

### [Blog 2 - AWS Shared Responsibility Model](3.2-Blog2/)
An analytical breakdown distinguishing AWS responsibilities (**"Security of the Cloud"**) from customer responsibilities (**"Security in the Cloud"**), illustrating how responsibilities shift across service models (from Amazon EC2 to managed services like Amazon S3) along with a practical system design checklist.

### [Blog 3 - AWS Security: Starting with Identity and Access Control (IAM & CloudTrail)](3.3-Blog3/)
A technical guide covering four essential identity and access management practices aligned with the **AWS Well-Architected Security Pillar**: protecting the Root user with MFA, adopting identity federation and temporary credentials (IAM Roles), enforcing **Least Privilege** down to S3 object prefixes, and auditing account activity with **AWS CloudTrail**.
